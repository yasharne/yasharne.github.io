About

Technical depth with a management surface area.

My background is infrastructure and systems engineering: Terraform, Kubernetes, cloud platforms, automation, observability, and the practical mechanics of keeping services alive.

As an engineering manager and MBA student, I care about clarity across both systems and organizations: what the system is doing, what the team owns, what risk looks like, and how technical decisions connect to business outcomes.
