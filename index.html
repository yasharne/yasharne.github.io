About

Clarity, ownership, and calm execution.

My technical background gives me the depth to challenge assumptions and understand risk. My role as a manager is broader: set direction, develop people, create useful constraints, and make the quality of decision-making scale beyond any one person.

I value direct communication, explicit ownership, evidence over ceremony, and operating rhythms that help teams surface problems early. Reliability matters because trust matters—to customers, colleagues, and the business.
